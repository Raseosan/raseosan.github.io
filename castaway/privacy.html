Castaway Island — Privacy Policy

Published by SO SUN Game Studio · Last updated 7 November 2026

Castaway Island is a single-player game: a season on an island, its challenges and its votes. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how to control it.

Play data (analytics)

To understand how people play and to find bugs, the game sends anonymous play data:

  • the buttons you press and the screens you visit;
  • events in your season: days, challenges, votes at council, who went out, how the season ended;
  • the game's own log lines (for example a slow frame, a warning or an error);
  • your device model, operating system, screen size, language and the game's version;
  • a random id made on your device the first time you play. It is not linked to your name, email or any account.

This data is processed by PostHog on servers in the European Union. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Share play data; when it is off, nothing is sent.

Messages you send us (“DM dev”)

The “DM dev” button lets you send a message to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you press Send to the developer. A message contains:

  • the title and description you write;
  • a screenshot of the game as it was when you opened the box (you can switch it off), and a picture from your photo library if you choose to add one;
  • the game's log for the current session, your device model and operating system, the game's version, the random id above and where you were in the game.

Messages are stored privately (Supabase, European Union), filed in a private project tracker on GitHub that only the developer can see, and emailed to the developer (sent through Resend). Please do not put personal information in a message or a picture you do not want us to see.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and we do not use advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no account. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Purchases

Castaway Island does not currently sell anything. If a future version offers optional purchases, payment is handled by Apple or Google, and this policy will be updated first.

Your choices

  • Turn play data off in Settings → Share play data.
  • Your season is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it, and a new random id is made if you install again.
  • To have your data or a message you sent deleted, email us with the date and your device model and we will remove it.

Children

Castaway Island is meant for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
