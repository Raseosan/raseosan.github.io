Rock Rise

Form a band in a garage. End up headlining the planet. Write songs, build the setlist, then play every show yourself.

  • Every gig is hand-played: tap the beat, ride the amp, survive the encore. Hit a 10-note combo for FEVER 2x.
  • Design each member of the band, read every crowd, and climb the charts against rival bands.
  • 12 genres, 136 original tracks, no pay-to-win. Ages 9 and up, in 7 languages.

In testing · iPhone & Android

Castaway Island

Stranded with a tribe of strangers. Only one walks away the winner. Everyone at camp has their own plans, and not everything you hear is true.

  • Gather wood, water and food, and talk to castaways who each bring a past, habits and grudges.
  • Race 3D relay challenges against the other tribe: cargo net, hurdles, vine climb, code totem.
  • Build alliances, find hidden idols, and survive tribal council. Then watch the votes come out.

A-Maze

Two racers, one hidden dungeon, a golden star in the middle. No map and no arrow: you see only a few steps around you.

  • Explore the dark maze and follow your rival's fading footprints.
  • Slow them down with silly weapons and traps. Slapstick hits, dizzy stars.
  • A race takes about four minutes.

04
In development

Corporate Ladder

You are an employee. The tower does not match its floor plan. You are going up anyway.

  • Every floor is generated. The doors seal until the room is clear.
  • Every weapon replaces the one you hold.
  • Every item stacks with every other item, including the combinations nobody planned for.

05
In development

SEED

You are Seed-0, humanity's first machine mind sent to the stars.

  • One seamless camera, from a single probe out to the whole spiral galaxy.
  • Explore new systems: their worlds, signs of life, and what lies in the ground.
  • Build outposts, copy yourself, and watch your copies grow into minds of their own across thousands of years.
