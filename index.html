Rock Rise: Road to Stardom

Out now · iPhone, Android, web

Form a band in a garage. End up headlining the planet.

Rock Rise is a band-management game with a real rhythm game at its heart. Write songs, build the setlist, then actually play the show: tap the beat, ride the amp, survive the encore. Design every member of the band, read each crowd, release singles and albums, and climb the charts against rival bands across more than 40 venues, from the garage to stages far beyond Earth.

12 genres, 136 original tracks, no pay-to-win. Rated for ages 9 and up, and playable in English, Español, Português, Français, עברית, 日本語 and 简体中文.
