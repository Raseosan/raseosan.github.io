# ROCK RISE

**Pitch:** Form a band in a garage, then actually play the show: a band-management sim with a real rhythm game at its heart.

- **Genre:** Band-management sim / rhythm game
- **Platforms:** Web (installable PWA), Android
- **Orientation:** Portrait (mobile-first)
- **Play:** https://raseosan.github.io/rock-rise-app/

## Features
- Every gig is hand-played: tap, hold, double-tap, ride the wave and dodge trap notes while the crowd-energy meter watches. Land a 10-note combo and the show goes into FEVER 2x.
- Build a band with opinions. Pick from 12 genres (each with its own perk and sound), design every member, then deal with their quirks, chemistry and feuds.
- Climb from Mom's Garage to Colossus Stadium across 22 venues, and write, record and release songs that climb the charts against rival bands.
