# Castaway Island

**Pitch:** Eighteen strangers, one island, one survivor: live a whole reality-show season of camp life, challenges, alliances and tribal council votes.

- **Genre:** Single-player social strategy / survival reality-show sim
- **Platforms:** Android (Google Play, in early testing), Windows, WebGL (Unity)
- **Orientation:** Landscape (1280x720)
- **Public URL:** none found yet (privacy policy: https://raseosan.github.io/castaway/privacy.html)

## Features
- Live at camp: gather firewood, water and food, build the shelter, and talk to castaways who each have their own past, habits and grudges.
- Race in 3D relay immunity challenges (cargo net, hurdles and limbo, vine climb, code totem) against the other tribe.
- Survive tribal council by answering the host, reading the room and casting your vote, then watch the votes revealed one by one.
