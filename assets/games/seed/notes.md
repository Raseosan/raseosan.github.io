# PROJECT SEED

**Pitch:** You are Seed-0, humanity's first autonomous interstellar synthetic intelligence: explore, replicate and diverge across a whole galaxy over deep time.

- **Genre:** Space exploration / grand simulation / strategy sandbox
- **Platforms:** PC (Windows)
- **Orientation:** Landscape
- **Status:** In development

## Features
- One seamless camera from a single probe out to the whole spiral galaxy and its neighbours: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
- Select any star to inspect it: its worlds, signs of life and the resources in the ground. Then travel there or enter the system to watch its planets orbit.
- Time is the main mechanic. Pause it or run it faster while voyages take decades, then found outposts and Branch new minds that share your memories.
